# PP Auto Spa Website

A responsive, static website for PP Auto Spa, a car-wash and detailing business in Thembisa. The site is built with HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript and can be hosted on any static web host.

## Pages

- `home.html` — home page and service highlights
- `our-story.html` — business introduction
- `services-and-prices.html` — wash and detailing services with starting prices
- `book-a-wash.html` — booking request form
- `index.html` — contact details, map, and message form

## Contact and booking

The booking and contact forms open a pre-filled WhatsApp message to **060 374 9813**. The visitor reviews and sends the message in WhatsApp; the website does not submit or store form data, and a booking is not confirmed until the business replies.

Service prices are displayed as starting prices and should be reviewed before publishing. Update the details in the HTML pages if the business name, contact information, services, or prices change.

## Local preview

Open `index.html` in a browser to view the contact page, or `home.html` to view the home page. The site uses Google Fonts and Google Maps, which require an internet connection; the rest of the site is static.

## Before publishing

Replace the `yourwebsite.com` placeholder URLs in `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` with the website's real public domain. Review the contact details, pricing, and service descriptions before launch.
